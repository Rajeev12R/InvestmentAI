# PHASE 13 — INVESTMENT PROCESS MODEL & 2x2 DECISION MATRIX

## 1. Decision Quality vs. Investment Outcome

The core institutional tenet of Phase 13 is:
> **Decision Quality $\ne$ Investment Outcome**

Financial markets contain irreducible noise, macro shocks, regime changes, and black-swan events. A high-quality process does not guarantee a positive return on every trade, nor does a profitable outcome prove investment skill.

## 2. Thesis Lifecycle & State Transitions

The thesis state machine is strictly decoupled from stock price fluctuations:

- **`WORKING`**: The foundational drivers and evidence remain intact.
- **`STRENGTHENING`**: Fundamental metrics exceed the original forecast trajectory.
- **`WEAKENING`**: Fundamental drivers decelerate or contradict expectations.
- **`BROKEN`**: Explicit falsification triggers or breakers have occurred.
- **`VALIDATED`**: Long-term strategic catalyst or fundamental target fully realized.
- **`INSUFFICIENT_DATA`**: Essential underlying facts cannot be verified.

### Key Invariant
- **`WORKING + LOSS`**: An asset whose operating fundamentals (margins, cash flow, debt reduction) are executing perfectly according to thesis, but whose stock price dropped due to broader market rate hikes.
- **`BROKEN + PROFIT`**: An asset whose operational thesis failed (margins collapsed, key product delayed), yet the stock price rallied due to a speculative meme surge or acquisition rumors.

Price movement alone can NEVER validate or falsify a fundamental thesis.
